Frequently Asked Questions

How does this differ from Revolut <18?
Revolut <18 focuses on spending and limits peer-to-peer or relative transfers on basic plans. Our app links directly to classroom learning, supports multi-relative deposits without extra barriers, and includes structured chore workflows.
How much does it cost?
Unlike services charging per-child fees (£3.99–£5.99/mo per child) or extra top-up charges, we offer a transparent flat family tier model with no hidden transaction fees.
Is my child's money safe and regulated?
Yes, fully backed by an established Irish pillar bank under EU banking regulations and European deposit protection standards.
What ages is this suitable for?
